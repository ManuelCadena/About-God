Project Sync / Status Update Summary 
Research Highlights for 2024 
Significant progress on anthrobots research, particularly in tracking communication between embryos to understand collective intelligence above the cellular level 
New work examining gene regulatory networks and their problem-solving capabilities below the cell level 
Upcoming publications on novel transcriptomics of anthrobots and xenobots, developed primarily in 2024 
Self-Improvising Memory Paper Discussion 
Core Thesis and Caterpillar-Butterfly Paradigm 
Central Observation: Caterpillars trained on specific tasks retain memories after metamorphosis into butterflies, despite massive brain remodeling 
Most neural connections are broken and many cells die during transformation 
The butterfly remembers original information but must remap it to completely new embodiment and behaviors 
This represents information survival through massive substrate refactoring 
Key Insight: The remarkable feature isn't maintaining information fidelity, but creatively remapping memories onto a completely new type of being with different problems and capabilities 
The Bow Tie Architecture 
Structure: Resembles an autoencoder with a large input funnel, thin middle layer, and large output expansion 
Function: Forces compression and generalization rather than detailed storage 
Input: Specific stimuli and experiences with detailed information 
Compression: Generalizes instances into simple generative kernels capturing essential patterns 
Output: Creative reinterpretation and expansion for current context 
Biological Examples: 
Signaling networks where multiple inputs converge on calcium fluxes, then fan out to diverse outputs 
Evolutionary processes where DNA information gets reinterpreted in new contexts 
Memory as Communication from Past Self 
Memories function as messages from previous versions of oneself rather than static storage 
Current actions and learning create messages for future self 
Challenge: Past self is inaccessible except through memory traces (engrams) that must be constantly reinterpreted 
Biological Reality: Working with fundamentally unreliable medium where parts change, mutate, and face errors 
Memories as Agents Concept 
Agency Spectrum: Agency exists on a continuum from fleeting thoughts to persistent patterns to full personalities 
Examples of Agential Patterns: 
Fleeting thoughts that disappear quickly 
Persistent/recurring thoughts that modify brain structure to facilitate their own continuation 
Dissociative identity fragments with goals and communication abilities 
Full human personalities 
Niche Construction: Some thought patterns actively change the brain to make their continuation easier, demonstrating self-preserving behavior 
Upcoming Research Directions 
Transcriptomics Studies 
New findings show xenobots and anthrobots have radically different transcriptomes compared to tissue of origin 
Same DNA but different embodiment leads to novel interpretation of genetic information 
Demonstrates how organisms reinterpret evolutionary affordances for new functions and behaviors 
Bioelectric Patterns as Thoughts 
Exploring bioelectric patterns during regeneration as literal thoughts of morphogenetic collective intelligence 
Investigating whether physical body serves as memory medium while bioelectric patterns act as driving agents 
Testing applications in regenerative medicine through this reversed perspective 
Action Items 
@Team: Continue development of transcriptomics research on anthrobots and xenobots for upcoming publication 
@ResearchGroup: Explore applications of bow tie architecture concepts to regenerative medicine 
@Collaborators: Investigate memory pattern agency in health and disease contexts
