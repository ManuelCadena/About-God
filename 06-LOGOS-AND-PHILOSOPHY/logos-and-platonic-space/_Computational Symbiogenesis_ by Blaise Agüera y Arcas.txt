Functionalism and the Nature of Life

Von Neumann's Universal Constructor Theory 
Self-Replication Requirements 
Von Neumann's analysis of self-replicating systems identifies four essential components: an instruction tape containing building instructions, a universal constructor that follows these instructions, a tape copier for offspring inheritance, and the requirement that constructor and copier instructions exist on the tape itself ‎‎ 00:20:25

Embodied vs. Disembodied Computation 
The critical distinction between Turing and von Neumann approaches lies in embodiment. Turing machines manipulate abstract symbols distinct from their physical substrate, preventing self-construction ‎‎ 00:22:14

Artificial Life Experiments 
Brainfuck-Based Evolution System 
The speaker presents original artificial life experiments using a modified Brainfuck programming language - a Turing-complete system with only eight instructions.
