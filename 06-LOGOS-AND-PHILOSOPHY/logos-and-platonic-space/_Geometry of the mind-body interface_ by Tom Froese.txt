Theoretical Framework: Irruption Theory 
Background and Motivation

Core Axioms of Irruption Theory 
An agent's motivations, as such, make a difference to the material basis of the agent's behavior ‎‎ 00:03:25
. This axiom establishes that mental states have causal power in determining behavioral outcomes. 
The theory acknowledges that motivational efficacy cannot be directly measured in laboratory settings ‎‎ 00:03:56
. This creates a fundamental gap between mental causation and empirical observation that cannot be resolved by simply imputing representational content to measured phenomena ‎‎ 00:04:30

The materiality of any situation does not provide a complete picture of reality and is inherently underdetermined ‎‎ 00:05:14
. This underdetermination opens degrees of freedom for motivational efficacy to play a causal role in observed phenomena ‎‎ 00:05:29

Mind-Body Interface Operators 
Two Directional Operators 
The theory proposes two operators depending on the direction of influence across the mind-body interface ‎‎ 00:05:51

From the measurement perspective, consequences appear without measurable causes 
Manifests as spontaneous or unexpected events in recordings ‎‎ 00:06:34

Empirical Evidence for Operators

Methodological Implications

Architectural Framework 
The overall architecture features two operators separated by a "black box middle" representing the explanatory gap ‎‎ 00:11:15

Geometric and Mathematical Constraints 
Interface Requirements 
Relationships across the mind-body interface must be expressed in dimensionless terms - pure numbers, unitless constants, and ratios - to avoid unit incompatibility between mental and physical domains ‎‎ 00:13:46
. The theory predicts multiplicative rather than additive relationships and emphasizes symmetrical operations to avoid energy conservation violations ‎‎ 00:14:40

Euler's Number (E) as Interface Constant 
Euler's number emerges as a special conversion constant because it is the only number whose rate of change equals itself, providing a unique way to convert between rates and quantities ‎‎ 00:15:23
. This mathematical property makes it ideal for mind-body interface operations. 
Experimental Validation 
Experimental Design 
Froese presents data from a social awareness study where participants interacted through a virtual interface, distinguishing between haptic feedback and socially mediated feedback ‎‎ 00:20:21
. The task required participants to click when they believed they were interacting with their partner. 
Key Findings 
Click response probability density peaks just after the total time horizon divided by E, corresponding to the optimal waiting time from decision theory ‎‎ 00:22:12
. This demonstrates that participants naturally organize their responses according to the secretary problem's 37% rule (1/E ≈ 0.37). 
Average haptic feedback follows an exponential increase to asymptote, with parameters A = 1/E and K = 1-1/E, exactly matching the proportions predicted by optimal decision theory ‎‎ 00:23:26

Global scalp potential, averaged across all channels, trials, and participants (over 1000 trials), follows a Poisson P1 curve with lambda = E ‎‎ 00:26:00

Respiratory Activity: Follows P1 curve pattern, showing compression until optimal point, then relaxation ‎‎ 00:27:12
Electrodermal Activity: Follows P0 survival curve (E^(-E×t)), providing the decay rate for other processes ‎‎ 00:28:15
Cardiac Timing: Ratio of blood pressure phase to total cardiac cycle arranges to hit 1/E boundary during trials ‎‎ 00:36:13
Perceptual Awareness Data 
Analysis of over 1000 perceptual awareness ratings reveals highest clarity before the optimal waiting time (observation phase), with systematic decreases afterward (action phase) ‎‎ 00:30:26

Frequency Band Analysis 
EEG Frequency Derivation

Philosophical Integration 
Middle Way Approach 
Drawing inspiration from early Chinese Buddhism, Froese proposes that conventional reality, Buddha nature/emptiness, and their intersection are all equally real ‎‎ 00:12:14

Alternative to Platonic Realism 
Rather than geometric forms existing in an independent Platonic realm, the theory suggests they emerge from and operate at the mind-body interface ‎‎ 00:12:38

Research Implications 
Multi-Scale Alignment

Future Directions 
The framework opens new avenues for investigating consciousness, agency, and embodied cognition through precise mathematical signatures while maintaining the irreducible reality of mental phenomena. The theory provides both conceptual clarity and empirical predictions for neuroscience research programs.
