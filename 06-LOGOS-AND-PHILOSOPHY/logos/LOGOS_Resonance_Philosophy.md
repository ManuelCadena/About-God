The computational core operates on a dual-attractor model. At the center burns a golden core — the A⁺ attractor — whose gravitational pull scales with the Lambda parameter. Surrounding it, the void of A⁻ exerts its own dark gravity: the entropy function that scatters, dissipates, and fragments. Every particle experiences both forces simultaneously. The beauty of the algorithm emerges from this tension — particles that achieve alignment trace luminous golden arcs near the center, while those caught in entropy carve erratic purple trajectories through the periphery. This is not random motion decorated with labels; it is a *painstakingly optimized force-balance system* where every coefficient has been refined through countless iterations to produce emergent beauty that feels both mathematical and organic.

Each exerts a gentle orbital influence on nearby particles, creating localized vortices of domain-colored energy. Concentric rings represent the seven layers of the C²AI framework, from Friston's homeostatic base to the Logos crown. Vesica Piscis intersections encode the overlap zones where domains interact. This geometric scaffold is not decorative — it is the *information architecture of consciousness itself*, expressed through computational relationships that a master of generative art would labor over for months to achieve the precise balance between structure and freedom.

The system never reaches equilibrium. Like consciousness itself, it is perpetually in process — a dynamic steady state where particles are born at the edges, flow through the field of forces, trace their unique trajectories through the domain nodes, and ultimately expire, leaving behind luminous trails that accumulate into density maps of alignment. The longer the algorithm runs, the more a mandala-like pattern emerges — not because it was designed, but because the forces *inevitably* converge on coherent structures. This emergent mandala is the product of deep computational expertise: each noise octave, each damping coefficient, each spawn distribution was calibrated with the precision of someone at the absolute top of their field in computational aesthetics.
