## Transcripción / Resumen

Summary 
This interview mainly discussed the free energy principle and its applications in neuroscience and artificial intelligence. Neil deGrasse Tyson hosted the Star Talk special edition with co-hosts Chuck Nice and Gary O'Reilly, featuring guest Carl Friston, a leading neuroscientist from University College London. Friston explained that the free energy principle is a mathematical prescription describing how things behave, particularly how the brain processes information and makes sense of the world. He described how our brains create models of reality based on sensory inputs and constantly update these models through active inference. The discussion explored how humans perceive reality through prediction and error correction, with Friston explaining that intelligence involves selecting which information to attend to while ignoring irrelevant data. The conversation then shifted to comparing human intelligence with artificial intelligence, particularly large language models, with Friston noting that while AI can process vast amounts of data, it lacks the embodied experience and selective attention that characterizes human intelligence. The hosts and guest also discussed consciousness, self-awareness, and psychiatric disorders as inference problems. Friston advocated for more sustainable, biomimetic approaches to AI development that would be more energy-efficient and closer to natural intelligence, moving from artificial intelligence (AI) to intelligent agents (IA).
